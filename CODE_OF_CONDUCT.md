# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AgroTech AI welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Give actionable feedback about code, documentation, and ideas.
- Respect different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Protect personal data, credentials, and private project information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, publishing private information without consent, and sharing credentials or sensitive data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Repeated or serious violations may result in a permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). Do not use the public issue tracker for sensitive reports.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chats, events, and any other forum where a person represents the project.
