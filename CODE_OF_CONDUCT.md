# 🌍 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AgroTech AI aims to provide a respectful, constructive, and inclusive environment for people learning, building, and improving agricultural technology. Participation should remain professional and focused on evidence and the work.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give technical feedback about the code, data, models, and outcomes rather than attacking people.
- Explain disagreement clearly and with evidence.
- Accept corrections and update documentation when claims are inaccurate.
- Protect user privacy and avoid sharing sensitive agricultural or personal data.
- Keep discussions constructive and relevant.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing private user records, farm locations, credentials, or confidential images.
- Deliberate disruption or repeated bad-faith behavior.
- Misrepresenting model output as guaranteed diagnosis or official agricultural guidance.
- Encouraging unsafe use of pesticides, fertilizers, food-safety classifiers, or other consequential predictions without expert review.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Actions should be proportionate to the issue and based on available context.

## Reporting

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Include enough context to investigate, but do not share unrelated private information.

Reports will be considered in good faith and kept confidential where practical. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository spaces and project-related communication, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
